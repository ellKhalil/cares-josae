About The Centre

Empowering Farmers & Innovators Through Applied Science

The Centre for Agricultural Research and Extension Services (CARES) was established at Federal University Dutse, Jigawa State to spearhead high-impact agricultural research, biotechnology adoption, soil health optimization, and modern extension methodologies.

CARES acts as the vibrant bridge connecting academic researchers, smallholder farmers, agribusiness enterprises, and policymakers—fostering resilience against climate change and promoting food security across Nigeria and the Sub-Saharan region.

Our Core Pillars

Key CARES Research & Extension Thrusts

Targeted solutions addressing pressing challenges in Nigerian agriculture, pastoral systems, and rural communities.

Soil Fertility & Plant Nutrition

Investigating organic amendments, cow dung & urea integration, nitrogen use efficiency (NUE), and soil microbial health in savanna Alfisols.

Smart Agritech & Precision Farming

Harnessing ICTs, sensor-based monitoring, GIS spatial mapping, and digital extension tools to optimize resource allocation for smallholders.

Agricultural Extension & Rural Gender

Analyzing determinants of extension access among female farmers, community-driven social development (CSDP), and participatory training.

Animal Science & Ruminant Nutrition

Alternative livestock feed formulating using indigenous fodder crops like Piliostigma thoningii (Kalgo) to sustain Kano Brown bucks.

Climate Adaptation Strategies

Evaluating smallholder adaptation mechanisms, drought-tolerant crop varieties, cassava processing tech, and media coverage of environmental issues.

Agribusiness & Rural Welfare

Assessing household income diversification, non-farm activity linkages, vegetable value chains, and entrepreneurship development.
